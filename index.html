Types of Child Abuse

hays

Physical Abuse

The punishment is too harsh, The child is often weak, sick, or tired due to lack of proper care.

😢

Emotional Abuse

Are constantly shouted at, insulted, or humiliated, Recieve no affection or attention from parents or guardians.

🚫

Sexual Abuse

Sexual abuse means any sexual act or touching of a child that happens without the child's consent or understanding. and also it can be done by an adult or an older person, and it causes harm to the child's body , mind, and emotions.

🏠

Emotional Neglect

Not showing love, attention, or emotional support to the child.

📱

Online Harassment

It often happens on Facebook, Tiktok, Messenger, Instagram, or online games-where a person is builled, mocked, or scared through messages, post, comments or pictures.

👥

Bullying

Unwanted aggressive behavior among school-aged children that involves a real or perceived power imbalance.

Resources and What to Do

Recognizing Signs of Abuse

Look for unexplained injuries, sudden behavior changes, fear of certain people or places, age-inappropriate sexual behavior, or poor hygiene.

How to Report Suspected Abuse

Barangay - Go to the barangay hall and report to the tanod or barangay captain

DSWD (Department of Social Welfare and Development)

Hotline: 1343 Actionline Against human Trafficking

PNP Women and Children - Protection Desk (WCPD) 117 (National Police Hotline)

Supporting a Child Who Discloses Abuse

Remain calm, believe the child, reassure them it's not their fault, avoid questioning details, and report to authorities immediately.

Prevention Strategies

Teach children about body safety, establish open communication, monitor online activity, know the warning signs, and educate yourself and others.
